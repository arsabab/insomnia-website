About the band

INSOMNIA is a Bangla rock band from Dhaka, formed in 2026.

We started with a dream, a passion for music, and a little bit of madness. Now, we’re working on our original songs, exploring different genres, and creating a sound that’s truly our own.

We believe music has no boundaries. It’s our sound, our madness, and our vibe that make us different. We’re not here to fit into a genre. We’re here to create our own.
